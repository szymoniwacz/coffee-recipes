## Extraction Logic

- **Full immersion, no bloom:** immediate closed-valve pour outperformed experiment 1 (bloom + 24 clicks) — more flavor, less dilution.
- **23 clicks:** best logged grind so far — balance between experiment 1 (24, thin) and experiment 3 (22, bitter).
- **Valve open at 1:30:** consistent timing reused in experiment 3; drawdown ~30 s to ~2:00.
- **140 g at 1:14:** clearer flavor but cup still felt slightly diluted — motivates experiment 4 at 120 g.

## Tuning Guide

| Goal | Change |
|---|---|
| More sweetness | reduce water to 120 g — see [full immersion, 23 clicks, 120 g](./full-immersion-23-clicks-120g.md); post-drawdown rest (experiment 4) |
| More body | reduce water to 120 g; keep 23 clicks |
| More clarity | not tested — shorter immersion or earlier release |
| More strength | less water (experiment 4); do not go finer than 22 without bitterness check |
| Less bitterness | stay at 23 clicks or coarser — experiment 3 at 22 showed light bitterness |
| Less acidity | not documented |
| Faster drawdown | coarser grind — risks dilution (experiment 1) |
| Slower extraction | finer grind — risks bitterness (experiment 3) |

## Warning Signs

| Symptom | Likely cause | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Watery / hollow | too coarse or too much water | keep 23 clicks; reduce water to 120 g |
| Muddy / heavy | overextraction — too fine or too long closed | coarser; open at 1:30 |
| Bitter / dry | too fine (experiment 3 at 22 clicks) | coarser to 23–24 clicks |
| Sour / sharp | underextraction | slightly finer; ensure full immersion to 1:30 |
| Drawdown too slow | too fine | coarser within 23–24 range |
| Diluted / thin body | too much water (140 g) | reduce to 120 g — experiment 4 |
