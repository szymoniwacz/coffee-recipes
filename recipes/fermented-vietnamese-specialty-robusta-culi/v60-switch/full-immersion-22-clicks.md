## Extraction Logic

- **22 clicks:** finer than experiment 2 (23) — more pronounced cup but light bitterness; upper bound for grind on this coffee at 140 g.
- **Full immersion, no bloom:** same workflow as experiment 2; grind change drove intensity and bitterness.
- **140 g at 1:14:** still felt slightly diluted despite finer grind — supports reducing water rather than grinding finer.
- **Valve open at 1:30:** same release timing as experiment 2.

## Tuning Guide

| Goal | Change |
|---|---|
| More sweetness | coarser to 23 clicks; reduce water — see [full immersion, 23 clicks, 120 g](./full-immersion-23-clicks-120g.md) |
| More body | reduce water to 120 g at 23 clicks — avoid going finer than 22 |
| More clarity | not tested |
| More strength | less water rather than finer than 22 clicks |
| Less bitterness | coarser to 23 clicks (experiment 2 had none); do not use 22 clicks |
| Less acidity | not documented |
| Faster drawdown | coarser — 23–24 clicks |
| Slower extraction | finer — risks bitterness at 22 clicks and below |
