# Aeropress — Paper filter

## Quick Brew Card

- **Dose:** 15 g · **Water:** 150 g · **Grind:** Comandante 18 clicks · **Temp:** TBD
- **Steep:** 0:00–~2:00 · **Press:** ~2:00, slow · **Total:** TBD
- **Pour schedule:** Bloom 30 g → +120 g (150 g) · light stir after top-up

## Short Version

1. Bloom 30 g for 30–40 s; top up to 150 g.
2. Light stir; steep ~2 min before pressing.
3. Slow plunge from ~2:00.

## Extraction Logic

- **1:10, 18 clicks** — same as Prism line; taste change mainly via **paper filter**.
- **Light stir + ~2 min before press:** calmer than [Killer](../aeropress-prism/killer.md); longer steep than [Sweet & Clean](../aeropress-prism/sweet-clean.md) (1:50 press).
- **Paper vs Prism:** cleaner, less body, milder, less oily.

## Warning Signs

| Symptom | Likely cause | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Too clean / thin | underextraction — short 2 min, too coarse | finer; longer before press |
| Too weak | underextraction | finer; longer steep |
| Bitter / dry | overextraction | shorter steep; coarser |
| Sour / thin | underextraction | finer |
| Hard to press | too fine | coarser |
| Paper taste | filter / no rinse | rinse filter — not tested |
