## Extraction Logic

- **21 clicks:** one click finer than 20-click variant — documented higher sweetness.
- **Stir at start + 20 s:** even extraction; second stir matches timing used in [Stable + bite](./stable-bite.md).

- **Metal + cloth:** same dual-filter setup as 20-click variant.

## Warning Signs

| Symptom | Likely cause | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Too clean / thin | underextraction — too coarse | finer; longer contact |
| Too heavy / muddy | overextraction — too fine | coarser (20 clicks) |
| Bitter / dry | overextraction — 21 clicks, long contact | coarser; shorten contact |
| Sour / sharp | underextraction | finer; longer contact |
| Drawdown too slow | too fine | coarser (20 clicks) |
| Weak aroma | tasted too hot | wait for ~60°C |
