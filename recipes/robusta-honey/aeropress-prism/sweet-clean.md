# Aeropress Prism — Sweet & Clean

## Quick Brew Card

- **Dose:** 15 g · **Water:** 150 g · **Grind:** Comandante 18 clicks · **Temp:** 92°C
- **Steep:** 0:00–1:50 · **Press:** start 1:50, 20–40 s · **Total:** 2:10–2:30
- **Prism:** metal filter · no bypass · bloom 30 g → +120 g (150 g) · 2 gentle stirs after top-up

## Short Version

1. Bloom 30 g for 30–40 s; top up to 150 g.
2. Two gentle stirs; place plunger. Steep until 1:50.
3. Press 20–40 s. Finish by 2:10–2:30.

## Extraction Logic

- **1:10** — higher concentration than V60 Switch (1:12.7) and syphon (1:13.3).
- **92°C + 18 clicks:** paired in source for this line.
- **2 gentle stirs:** limited agitation → clarity and sweetness.
- **Press from 1:50, 20–40 s:** earlier / shorter press than [Killer](./killer.md).

## Tuning Guide

| Goal | Change |
|---|---|
| More sweetness | gentler stirring; press at 1:50 with shorter plunge (20 s) |
| More body | slightly later / slower press — toward [Killer](./killer.md) |
| More strength | [Killer](./killer.md) |
| More clarity | [Paper filter](../aeropress-paper/paper-filter.md) |
| Less bitterness | earlier press; 92°C or lower; coarser than 18 — not tested |
| Less acidity | not documented |
| Less muddy cup | lighter stirring; earlier press |

## Warning Signs

| Symptom | Likely cause | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Too intense / harsh | overextraction — late / long press | earlier press; compare Killer |
| Too weak | underextraction — too coarse, short bloom | finer; longer before press |
| Bitter / dry | overextraction | earlier press; coarser |
| Sour / thin | underextraction | finer; longer bloom |
| Hard to press | too fine grind | coarser |
| Too oily / muddy | too strong stirring | keep 2 gentle stirs |
