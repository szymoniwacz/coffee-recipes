# V60 Switch — Immersion, sweetness + body

## Quick Brew Card

- **Dose:** 15 g · **Water:** 190 g · **Grind:** Comandante 24–25 clicks · **Temp:** 90°C
- **Valve:** closed · **Bloom:** 40 g / 45 s
- **Immersion / release:** closed 0:45–1:45 · open at 1:45 · total 2:30–2:50
- **Pour schedule:** Bloom 40 g (closed) → +150 g (190 g, closed) → release

## Short Version

1. Close valve. Bloom 40 g for 45 s.
2. Pour to 190 g total; keep valve closed until 1:45.
3. Open valve at 1:45; drain by 2:30–2:50.

## Extraction Logic

- **Closed Switch + pour to 190 g:** long water–coffee contact increases sweetness and body; limits pour-over clarity and acidity typical of short percolation.
- **90°C:** slightly below many 92–96°C recipes; aligns with very low acidity in Result.
- **24–25 clicks:** finer than syphon (20–21) and moka baseline (22); supports full immersion at 15 g / 190 g.
- **Bloom 40 g / 45 s:** wets bed before main 150 g pour.

## Tuning Guide

| Goal | Change |
|---|---|
| More sweetness | Longer closed time before 1:45 (watch bitterness); upper end of 24–25 clicks |
| More body | Full 190 g; hold closed to 1:45; slightly finer in range |
| More clarity | Shorter closed time; open earlier — not tested |
| More strength | Slightly finer or less total water — not tested |
| Less bitterness | Shorter immersion; coarser in 24–25 range; lower temperature |
| Less acidity | Already very low; keep 90°C and closed immersion |
| Faster drawdown | Coarser grind — not tested |
| Slower extraction | Longer before opening valve — not tested |

## Warning Signs

| Symptom | Likely cause | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Watery / hollow | underextraction — too coarse, too short closed, too cool | finer; longer closed (careful); slightly higher temp (careful) |
| Muddy / heavy | overextraction or too much agitation | shorter closed time; coarser; lighter stirring |
| Bitter / dry | overextraction — too fine, closed too long past 1:45 | coarser; open at 1:45; lower temp |
| Sour / sharp | underextraction | finer; ensure full 190 g and 60 s closed after main pour |
| Drawdown too slow | too fine or too many fines | coarser |
