# Electric moka pot — Current baseline

## Quick Brew Card

- **Dose:** 15 g · **Water:** 220 g hot · **Grind:** Comandante 22 clicks · **Start temp:** hot water in base
- **Target yield:** ~175 g
- **Shutdown:** first bubbling **or** when stream lightens

## Short Version

1. Add 220 g hot water to base; 15 g leveled coffee in basket (no tamp).
2. Start moka pot.
3. Shut off at first bubbling or when stream lightens (~175 g yield).

## Brewing Workflow

1. Pour 220 g hot water into the lower chamber.
2. Add 15 g coffee to the basket.
3. Level the coffee.
4. Do not tamp.
5. Start the moka pot.
6. Turn off at first bubbling **or** when the stream becomes lighter.

## Extraction Logic

- **22 clicks** — coarser than [stronger](./stronger-concentrated.md) (18) — lower risk of bitter over-extraction.
- **220 g hot water** — documented charge; more water than [stronger](./stronger-concentrated.md) (200 g) at same dose.
- **Hot water start** — documented workflow.
- **Shutoff at bubbling or stream lightening** — limits overheating.

## Tuning Guide

| Goal | Change |
|---|---|
| More sweetness | earlier shutoff (stream lightening); 22 clicks; avoid 18 clicks (stronger recipe) |
| More body | slightly finer than 22 — not tested |

## Warning Signs
