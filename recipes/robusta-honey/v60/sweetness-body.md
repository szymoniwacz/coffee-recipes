## Extraction Logic

- **190 g water:** lower total water than 200 g recipes → higher concentration (documented Result).
- **24 clicks:** same grind reference as [Balanced](./balanced.md); Switch uses 24–25 at 190 g with documented sweetness/body on immersion.
- **Four pours after bloom:** 45 + 40 + 40 + 35 g to 190 g — similar pour shape to [Stable](./stable.md) but scaled to 190 g.
